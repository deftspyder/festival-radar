How to Use Festival Radar

Festival Radar tracks electronic festivals and shows, warns you before tickets drop, and keeps your crew on the same page.

Getting Started
  • Sign in with Google or an email link. You'll confirm you're 18 or older once.
  • Join a crew from a friend's invite link, or start your own in Settings and invite people.
  • Add a photo and a short bio from Edit profile in My Radar, so your crew can put a face to a name.
Festivals and Picks
  • The Festivals tab lists events near you, around the USA and worldwide. Tap a festival name for its site and an artist for Spotify.
  • Know the name? Type it in the search box above the list. It searches every section, plus club shows and concerts.
  • Mark Going, Maybe or Pass on each card. Your crew sees your picks, and you see theirs.
  • Tap the heart to save a festival to My Radar without deciding yet.
  • Tap the share arrow on a card to text it to a friend (the link shows the festival's picture and dates), send it to your crew chat, or message it to a Festival Radar friend.
  • A glowing light on a card means tickets go on sale within a week. A dim light means the date isn't confirmed.
  • Calendar shows everything on a timeline. Tap a bar to open that festival.
My Radar and Alerts
  • My Radar collects the festivals you've hearted or marked Going or Maybe, plus upcoming presales for them.
  • Turn on alerts at the bottom of My Radar to get a heads-up 24 hours and 15 minutes before tickets go on sale. Tapping the alert takes you to tickets.
  • Next year: when a festival you went to ends, its next edition is added to your festivals automatically once it's announced, so you get its presale alerts. Undo it any time under Watching for next year. For festivals you only hearted or marked Maybe, My Radar asks "Watch for next year?" instead.
  • On iPhone, alerts need the site on your Home Screen: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from there.
  • My Artists: pick your favorite artists. Festivals with your crew's favorites on the lineup get highlighted. Can't find one? Type it in and send it to Bill.
Your Crew
  • The Crew tab shows what your crew is going to, festivals with two or more of you Going, everyone's favorite artists, and recent activity.
  • Crews are optional. Planning solo? Everything else works without one.
  • Start a crew from the Crew tab (you can start up to 5). It works right away and shows "Waiting for your first member" until a friend joins.
  • Invite friends from the Crew tab, the menu or Settings. Share invite link opens your phone's share sheet, so you can send it by text to people in your contacts. Anyone with the link can join after signing in.
  • In several crews? Pick one at the top of the Crew tab, or view all of them together.
Chats
  • Every crew has a group chat, and every festival has a crew chat and a public chat. Tap the chat bubble on a festival card, or open Chats in the Crew tab.
  • Marking Going adds you to that festival's crew chat (you can turn this off in Settings).
  • Public chats are seen by everyone on Festival Radar. Don't share where you're staying or other private details.
  • Use the ⋯ next to a message to delete your own, report one, or mute someone in public chats.
Friends and Messages
  • Met someone at a show? Open My code in My Radar and let them scan it with their phone camera. You're friends right away.
  • Not together? Use Share link instead. It works for 7 days.
  • Friends can message you and see your name, main photo and artists you both love. Only your crew sees which festivals you're going to.
  • From a friend's profile you can message them or invite them to your crew.
Privacy and Safety
  • Make your profile private in Edit profile: people outside your crews then see only your name.
  • Tap anyone's name or photo to see their profile, block them, or report them.
  • Your birthday is never shown. You choose whether your age shows.
  • Read the full privacy policy.
