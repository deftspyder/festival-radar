Festival Radar Privacy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Festival Radar is a small festival tracker run by Bill for friends who go to shows together. This page explains what it stores and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, used only to send you sign-in links.
  • The name you choose to show the crew.
  • Your picks (Going, Maybe, Pass), the festivals you heart, and your favorite artists.
  • Whether you opted in to emails about festival deals and presale offers. This is off unless you turn it on.

Who can see it

Your name, picks, hearted festivals and favorite artists are visible to members of your crews, so the crew can see who is going. Your email address is never shown to other members.

Deals and offers

We only email offers to people who opted in. We do not sell your email address. If we work with ticket sellers or promoters, offers are sent by Festival Radar, and your email is not handed to them without asking you first.

Where it is stored

Accounts and picks are stored with Supabase, our database provider, in the United States. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Ticket links take you to the festival or ticket seller's own site, which has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

To change your email preference or delete your account and everything in it, contact Bill. Deleting your account removes your picks, hearted festivals and favorite artists too.
