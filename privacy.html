Festival Radar Privacy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Festival Radar is a small festival tracker run by Bill for friends who go to shows together. This page explains what it stores and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, used only to send you sign-in links.
  • The name you choose to show the crew.
  • Your birthday, used once to confirm you are 18 or older. It is never shown to anyone. If you choose, your age (not your birthday) shows on your profile.
  • Anything you add to your profile: up to 3 photos, a short bio and your city. All optional. Photos are resized on your device and their location info and other hidden details are removed before upload.
  • People you block, and reports you send about a profile, photo or message.
  • Chat messages you post, which chats you follow, what you've read, and people you mute in public chats.
  • Your friends (people you add with your code or link), your friend codes, and direct messages.
  • Feedback and bug reports you send, with your browser, screen size and the page you were on only if you tick the box to include them. Only Bill sees feedback.
  • Your picks (Going, Maybe, Pass), the festivals you heart, and your favorite artists.
  • If you turn on presale alerts, a delivery address for each device you turn them on for (provided by your browser's push service). Turning alerts off on a device removes it.
  • Whether you opted in to emails about festival deals and presale offers. This is off unless you turn it on.

Who can see it

Your name, picks, hearted festivals and favorite artists are visible to members of your crews, so the crew can see who is going. Your crew also sees an activity feed of recent Going, Maybe and hearts, and crewmates who turned on crew pings may get a notification when you mark a festival Going. Your email address is never shown to other members.

Your crews see your full profile: photos, bio, city and age if you show it. Joining a crew lets its members see your profile, picks and favorites, and the site tells you this before you join. People outside your crews can see only your name and main photo (for example, in public festival chats), and if you make your profile private they see only your name. Someone you block can't see your profile, and you won't see theirs. Bill can see reports and the profiles and photos they are about, so he can remove anything that breaks the rules.

Chats

Crew chats, including each festival's crew chat, can be read only by current members of that crew. Public festival chats can be read by anyone signed in to Festival Radar, so don't share private details there like where you're staying. Signed-out visitors can't read any chat. Public chats close 30 days after the festival ends. You can delete your own messages at any time, and edit them for 15 minutes after sending. When you edit a message, the earlier version is kept privately so Bill can review it if the message is reported. Bill can remove messages, and remove people from public chats, when they break the rules. If you've turned on crew pings, new crew chat messages can send a notification to your device. In crew chats and direct messages, the people in that chat can see when you're typing (this isn't saved) and "Seen by" for messages you've read; you can turn off read receipts in Settings, and then you won't see theirs either.

Friends and direct messages

You can add a friend by scanning their code or opening their link; you're then friends both ways until either of you removes the other. Codes on screen change every 10 minutes and links last 7 days. Friends outside your crews see only your name, your main photo (unless your profile is private) and the artists you both love. They never see which festivals you're going to, your city, or the rest of your profile. Direct messages can be sent only between friends and crewmates, are seen only by the two of you, and stop between people who block each other. You can delete your own messages, and Bill can remove reported ones.

Deals and offers

We only email offers to people who opted in. We do not sell your email address. If we work with ticket sellers or promoters, offers are sent by Festival Radar, and your email is not handed to them without asking you first.

Where it is stored

Accounts and picks are stored with Supabase, our database provider, in the United States. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Ticket links take you to the festival or ticket seller's own site, which has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

To change your email preference or delete your account and everything in it, contact Bill. Deleting your account removes your picks, hearted festivals, favorite artists, profile, photos, birthday, friends and chat and direct messages too. You can remove any photo yourself at any time from Edit profile.
